<p>Apel is your own AI agent, and it runs entirely on your phone. Your chats, channels, files, skills, automations, databases and memory live in the app. You connect your own AI provider (OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible server) and pay it directly, only for what you use. There is no server, no account with the maker and no telemetry.</p>

<p><b>What it does</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Agents</b> for different jobs, each with its own name, look, model, instructions and memory. They can ask each other what they know, and hand big tasks to subagents with a fresh chat, always with your OK.</li>
<li><b>Chat</b> with streaming answers and the agent's steps as they happen; attach pictures, PDFs and text files; dictate; edit and ask again.</li>
<li><b>Channels</b> keep chats apart under their own name and colour.</li>
<li><b>Tools</b>, each Allowed, Ask first or Off: web search and reading pages, files in its workspace, memory, skills, automations, databases, notifications. Risky actions wait for your OK.</li>
<li><b>Remote tool servers</b> (MCP over HTTP), added with an address and an optional token, and an experimental hidden browser.</li>
<li><b>Automations</b>: scheduled jobs that run with the screen off, even after you close the app if you let it keep running in the background, and post their result to a channel.</li>
<li><b>Databases</b>: SQLite databases the agent can read or write under rules you set, with a table browser, a SQL console and CSV import and export.</li>
<li><b>Usage and budget</b>: what each model and each job cost, and a monthly limit.</li>
<li><b>Backup</b>: everything except keys and tokens in one zip, restored on a new phone in a few taps.</li>
<li><b>The mascot</b>: a blob you shape, colour and finish yourself, on Home and on a home-screen widget.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>Everything stays on the phone. The only network calls go to the AI provider you chose, to web pages when the agent searches or reads them, and to tool servers you add. Keys are kept in the Android keystore and never leave the address they belong to. By default, OpenRouter requests only go to providers that keep no data.</p>

<p>Apel needs an AI provider to answer. OpenRouter's free models work without paying anything, within their daily limits.</p>
